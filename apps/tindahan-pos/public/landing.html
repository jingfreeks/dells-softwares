The problem

A notebook can't tell you what you're losing.

Most small shops run on memory and a ledger. It works until the day you need to know why the money doesn't match the stock.

Stock runs out quietly

Your best seller is gone by noon and nobody notices until a customer asks. Every empty shelf is a sale that walked next door.

Utang gets forgotten

Credit is written on a page and settled from memory. Balances drift, old debts go unchased, and working capital sits in someone else's kitchen.

The drawer doesn't add up

Cash is short at closing and there's no way to tell whether it was a mistake, a missed charge, or something worse.

Everything the counter needs, nothing it doesn't.

Fast checkout

Questions

The things owners ask us first.

What happens when the internet goes down?

The register keeps working. Sales are saved on the device and upload automatically once you reconnect, so you never have to stop selling or fall back to paper.

Do I need a barcode scanner or special hardware?

No. Tindahan is built to be tapped, because most small-shop items have no barcode. Any reasonably recent Android tablet or laptop will do. If you already own a scanner or a thermal printer, both are supported.

Do I have to type in all my products?

No. You can start from a list of common items with prices already filled in and untick what you don't carry, scan what's on your shelf, or import a spreadsheet. Typing is the last resort, not the first.

Can my staff use it without seeing my sales figures?

Yes. Cashiers sign in with a PIN and see only their own shift total. Reports, margins, prices and settings are hidden from them, and actions like voiding a paid sale need your approval.

Is my data safe, and can I get it out?

Your sales history backs up automatically and you can export everything — sales, products, customers — as a spreadsheet at any time. You are never locked in.

Can it print official receipts?

It can print receipts with your business details, TIN and permit number. Whether those satisfy your BIR obligations depends on your registration type, so please confirm the current requirements with the BIR or your accountant — we can't advise on that.
